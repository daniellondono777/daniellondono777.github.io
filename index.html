About

Daniel Londoño (a.k.a Zephanya Giancana) is a security researcher specializing in cloud identity threats and offensive security. Currently working at Lumu Technologies , he builds detection systems that catch real attackers, analyzing behavioral signals across cloud environments to surface threats before they escalate.

His work spans application and cloud security. He reproduces critical CVEs, audits production pipelines, and maps adversary tradecraft across GCP and Entra ID environments.

Current research includes Living Off The Cloud (LOTC), a GTFOBins-style taxonomy of GCP attacker techniques, and published analyses covering TeaBot malware, npm supply chain attacks, and Entra ID lateral movement methodology.

Holds a Systems and Computing Engineering degree from Universidad de los Andes and a postgraduate degree in Cybersecurity from George Brown College, Toronto, Canada. Founder of Zafani Baixa Security, a nonprofit offering pro bono security assessments to underserved organizations. Credited for responsible disclosure in the financial sector.
